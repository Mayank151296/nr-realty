How to Verify a MahaRERA Registration Before You Buy

You verify a MahaRERA registration by searching the project's P-number directly on the MahaRERA portal at maharera.maharashtra.gov.in, reading the live record rather than a screenshot, and checking five fields: the promoter's legal name, the declared completion date, the sanctioned unit count and carpet areas, the registration status, and the complaints filed against the project. The check takes about five minutes and costs nothing.

Updated 2026-09-02 · Written by Om Shanti N R Construction, Palghar

Step by step: checking a registration in five minutes

  • Get the registration number. It begins with P and is followed by digits, for example P99000049283. Under MahaRERA rules in force since August 2023, it must appear on every advertisement, hoarding and brochure, alongside a QR code.
  • Go to the MahaRERA portal and open the project search. Search by registration number rather than by project name - names repeat, numbers do not.
  • Open the live record. Do not accept a PDF or a screenshot from the sales team. Records change; screenshots do not.
  • Read the five fields listed below.
  • Scan the QR code on the brochure yourself and confirm it resolves to the same record. A QR code that leads to a marketing page rather than the portal is a red flag.

What the five fields actually tell you

Field What to look for Why it matters
Promoter nameThe exact legal entity, and any co-promoters or land owners listedThis is who you have a contract with. If the name on the record differs from the name on the brochure, ask why before paying.
Registration statusActive, lapsed, or extendedA lapsed registration means the promoter cannot legally advertise or sell in that project until it is revived.
Proposed completion dateThe date declared on the portalThis is the date that carries legal weight, not the date given verbally. Compare the two.
Units and carpet areasNumber of units by configuration, and carpet area in sq.m.Cross-check against what you are being sold. Carpet area on the portal is the defined measure.
ComplaintsNumber and nature of complaints filedA pattern of possession-delay complaints is more informative than any brochure.

What a co-promoter or land owner entry means

Many Maharashtra projects involve two parties: a developer who builds, and a land owner or investor who contributes the land in return for a share of the area or the revenue. MahaRERA's Circular 13 of 2017 requires such a party to be registered and displayed on the project as a promoter in the land owner or investor category.

This matters to a buyer for a practical reason. If you are buying a unit from the land owner's share rather than from the developer, the entity you contract with is the land owner. You want that entity visible on the registration, and you want your agreement to reflect it. Ask directly whose inventory you are being sold.

Five things on the portal that should stop a booking

  • No registration number at all for a project that is being advertised and sold
  • A registration status that is lapsed, with sales continuing anyway
  • A promoter name on the record that does not match the entity asking you for money
  • A declared completion date materially later than what you were told verbally
  • A pattern of complaints about the same issue from multiple buyers

What verification does not cover

A live registration confirms that a project is registered and that certain particulars have been declared. It is not a quality certificate, it is not a guarantee of on-time delivery, and it does not replace a title search by your own advocate.

Run the portal check first because it is free and fast. Then commission the title certificate, read the sanctioned plan, and for a ready building, insist on seeing the occupation certificate.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I check a MahaRERA registration number?

On the official MahaRERA portal at maharera.maharashtra.gov.in, using the project search. Search by the P-number rather than the project name.

Is a MahaRERA QR code mandatory in Maharashtra?

Yes. Since August 2023 MahaRERA has required promoters to display the registration number and a QR code linking to the project record on advertisements and marketing material, with penalties for non-compliance.

What does 'Promoter (Land Owner / Investor)' mean on a MahaRERA record?

It identifies a party who contributed land or investment to the project in return for a share of area or revenue, and who is registered as a promoter under MahaRERA Circular 13 of 2017. If you are buying from that party's share, they are the entity you contract with.

Does MahaRERA registration guarantee the project will be delivered on time?

No. Registration means the project is registered and its particulars declared, including a completion date. It gives you a record and a complaints mechanism; it is not a delivery guarantee or a quality certificate.
