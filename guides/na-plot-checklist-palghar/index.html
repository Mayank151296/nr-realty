Buying an NA Plot in Palghar: The Document Checklist

Before buying an NA plot in Palghar, verify six things: the 7/12 extract and the chain of title, the non-agricultural (NA) order converting the land use, the sanctioned layout from the planning authority, legal access to the plot by a recorded road, the MahaRERA registration of the plotted scheme, and whether the internal infrastructure shown in the layout has actually been laid. A plot that fails on access or on NA conversion is not a bargain, it is a dispute.

Updated 2026-09-02 · Written by Om Shanti N R Construction, Palghar

Why plots need more diligence than flats

With an apartment you are buying into a structure that a planning authority has sanctioned and, if it is ready, certified. With a plot you are buying a piece of revenue land, and most of what determines its value is in records rather than in what you can see on site.

Land in Maharashtra is agricultural by default. Converting it to non-agricultural use requires an order from the collector. Until that order exists, a residential plot on that land cannot be legally developed for residential use, however convincing the layout drawing looks.

What to check on the ground

  • Walk the plot and match its boundaries against the layout and the survey map. Ask for a demarcation by a licensed surveyor if the boundaries are unclear.
  • Check whether internal roads exist and what they are made of. An RCC internal road is a different asset from a levelled mud track shown as a road on the layout.
  • Confirm water, electricity and drainage arrangements, and who is responsible for them after the sale.
  • Check the surrounding land use. A residential plot with an industrial or quarrying neighbour is a different proposition from what the brochure shows.
  • Ask about the CTS or survey number of adjoining plots and whether any are under litigation.

How payment should work

Pay by banking channel against a registered agreement, into the account named in the agreement. Register the sale deed and pay the stamp duty; an unregistered agreement gives you very little.

Be cautious about schemes that ask for a large advance before the NA order or the layout sanction is in hand. If the conversion or the sanction does not come through, your money is tied to someone else's approval process.

Frequently asked questions

What is an NA order and why does it matter?

A non-agricultural order is issued by the collector converting land from agricultural to non-agricultural use. Without it, land cannot be legally developed or used for residential purposes, regardless of how the layout is marketed.

Is RERA registration required for buying a plot in Maharashtra?

For plotted development and the sale of NA plots, yes. MahaRERA has held that such schemes fall within the Act and require registration, and has acted against promoters who sold plots without registering.

What is a 7/12 extract?

The 7/12 extract is the Maharashtra land record showing the survey number, area, current owner, tenure and any rights, loans or litigation recorded against the land. It is the starting point of any land title check.

What should I check about access to a plot?

That there is a legally recorded road giving access to the plot, shown in the sanctioned layout and reflected in records. A promised future road is not access, and a landlocked plot is very difficult to resell.
